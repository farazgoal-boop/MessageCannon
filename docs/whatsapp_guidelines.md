# WhatsApp Compliance & Guidelines

## Official WhatsApp Terms

MessageCannon users must comply with WhatsApp's Official Business Terms:

- No spam or unsolicited messages
- All recipients must have opted in
- No automated campaigns without consent
- Respect message sending rate limits

Reference: https://www.whatsapp.com/business/


## Legal Compliance by Country

### Pakistan

- Telemarketing Regulation Authority (TRA) guidelines apply
- Consent is mandatory before messaging
- Keep records of customer consent for 1 year
- Respect "Do Not Call" requests immediately

### General Requirements

1. **Consent**: Explicit opt-in from customers
2. **Records**: Maintain proof of consent
3. **Privacy**: Do not share customer data
4. **Unsubscribe**: Provide way to opt-out
5. **Identification**: Clearly identify yourself

## Best Practices

### Do's ✓

- ✓ Segment audiences (VIP, Regular, Pending)
- ✓ Personalize messages with customer names
- ✓ Use professional tone
- ✓ Include business contact information
- ✓ Monitor response rates
- ✓ Respect time zones
- ✓ Use reasonable delays between messages

### Don'ts ✗

- ✗ Send without explicit consent
- ✗ Spam or bulk unwanted messages
- ✗ Use automated voices/bots initially
- ✗ Message late night/early morning
- ✗ Share customer data with third parties
- ✗ Impersonate other businesses
- ✗ Use misleading or false information

## Message Rate Limits

**WhatsApp may temporarily block accounts that:**

- Send >10 messages in 1 minute
- Send >100 messages in 1 hour
- Send >1000 messages in 24 hours

**MessageCannon Safeguards:**

- Default 30-second delay between messages
- Optional ±5 second random jitter
- Maximum 50 messages per session
- Progress monitoring and pause/resume

## Account Safety

### Protect Your Account

1. Use strong password
2. Enable two-factor authentication
3. Keep WhatsApp updated
4. Don't share QR code
5. Monitor login activity
6. Review connected devices

### Signs of Account Compromise

- Unexpected logouts
- Messages you didn't send
- Slow response loading
- Account banned messages

## Consequences of Violation

- **Temporary ban**: 24-72 hours (first offense)
- **Permanent ban**: Repeated violations
- **Legal action**: In some jurisdictions
- **Business damage**: Loss of customer trust

## When You Get Blocked

If WhatsApp blocks your account:

1. **Wait 24 hours** - Try logging in again
2. **Contact WhatsApp Support** - Explain legitimate use
3. **Change number** - If permanent ban (last resort)
4. **Review practices** - Find what caused block

## Recommended Usage Pattern

For maximum safety:

```
- Send 30-40 messages per session
- Take breaks between campaigns
- Don't repeat same message to same group
- Vary sending times
- Monitor response rates
- Clean contact list (remove invalid numbers)
```

## Testing Before Full Campaign

1. Send to small group (5-10 contacts)
2. Monitor for any warnings
3. Wait 1-2 days
4. Proceed if no issues
5. Scale gradually
